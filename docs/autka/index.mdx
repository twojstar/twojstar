Autka is an open-source Android app that helps you shop for cars across borders without juggling twelve browser tabs. Formerly known as CarGate, it brings together search, filtering, currency comparison, and USA import cost calculation into one streamlined experience. Whether you are browsing Polish marketplaces, scouting European dealers, or researching US auction inventory, Autka keeps your search intent intact and your data private.

## What Autka does

Buying a car across borders means dealing with different marketplaces, currencies, and import rules. Autka unifies that process into a single app. When a licensed listing feed is available, the backend normalizes it into one catalogue. When a marketplace cannot be directly ingested, Autka preserves your search filters and hands you off to the original site through a deep link instead of scraping it.

## Privacy and transparency

Autka does not include ads or analytics SDKs. The Android manifest requests only internet and network-state permissions. Your search data stays on your device; the app does not track your browsing behavior or sell your data.

## Current project state

The Android app and Cloudflare backend are fully functional. However, Autka deliberately does not fabricate production inventory. Sample offers are available only in debug or demo builds. Until a compliant live data provider is connected, the internal production catalogue may be empty and marketplace deep-links serve as the main hand-off for unsupported sources.

This makes Autka useful today as an import-cost calculator and search companion, while the full aggregator vision depends on data partnerships.

## Looking for partners

The most valuable contribution to Autka right now is lawful access to listing data, not another scraper. Potential partners include:

- Marketplaces and aggregators able to license search or listing data
- Dealers and importers willing to provide authorized JSON, CSV, or API inventory feeds
- US auction buyers, brokers, and logistics partners who can provide realistic shipping and landed-cost inputs
- Connected-seller integrations where a business explicitly authorizes access to its own inventory
- Affiliate or lead-generation partnerships that can fund development without turning Autka into an ad farm

A first integration does not need to be large, but it does need enough structured data to normalize into Autka's offer model. See [Data Sources and Partnerships](/autka/data-sources) for the full partner checklist and integration paths.
